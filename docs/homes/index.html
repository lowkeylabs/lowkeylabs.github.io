Architectural Keepsakes

A home is more than a place. It's part of your story.

A Place Worth Remembering

Homes hold the everyday moments that become lasting memories: first arrivals, familiar rooms, gatherings, and goodbyes. Our architectural keepsakes celebrate the places that shape those stories, whether you're welcoming a new beginning or remembering a home you've loved.

The Art of Subtraction

Most painting creates an image by adding pigment. Laser ablation works in reverse. We prepare a canvas with layers of paint, then guide a precision laser across its surface to selectively remove the upper layer. The lighter tones beneath emerge as highlights, lines, and architectural details.

The result is not an inkjet print of a photograph. It is an image formed directly in the painted surface, through a carefully controlled process of removal and revelation.

Made with Care

Each piece begins with a reference photograph of the home. We thoughtfully prepare the composition, build up the painted surface, and tune the laser process to translate light, shadow, and detail onto canvas. The finished artwork is cleaned and inspected by hand.

Small-batch production gives us the freedom to attend to each home's distinctive character.

About Your Keepsake

The label on the back of your artwork records details about the piece and its making. It connects a particular home, a moment in time, and the craft behind the finished work.
