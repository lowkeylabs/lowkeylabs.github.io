Crystal Glass

Our Fan District ornaments are made from K5 crystal glass, chosen for its clarity and the way it catches and reflects light. Unlike a printed decoration, the design is engraved into the glass surface itself.

How the Engraving Is Made

Clear glass does not readily absorb the light from our engraving laser. We first use an airbrush to apply a thin, even mask of black tempera paint. The dark coating absorbs laser energy and helps transfer heat to the glass beneath.

As the laser traces the design, rapid localized heating creates tiny fractures at the glass surface. Together, these microscopic marks scatter light and make the engraved image appear frosted against the clear crystal.

After engraving, we wash away the temporary tempera coating, revealing the design in the glass. The black paint is a tool in the process, not part of the finished ornament.
